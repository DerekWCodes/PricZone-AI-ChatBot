PricZone Ask AI Visitor Information Agreement

By submitting your first name and email address through the PricZone Ask AI access form, you agree that PricZone may use the information you provide to send Ask AI access emails, site notifications, and feedback requests related to your use of this tool.

What this tool is for

PricZone Ask AI is provided to help visitors interact with our website, receive shopping assistance, discover products, and request access to the Ask AI experience.

What information we collect

  • Your first name
  • Your email address
  • Your agreement to this usage statement
  • Basic submission details such as the page where the request was made and the time of submission

How we use your information

  • To send Ask AI access emails
  • To send site-related notifications connected to the Ask AI experience
  • To send feedback requests about your experience with Ask AI
  • To manage visitor access requests and prevent repeated submissions while an active saved request remains on file

What we will not do with your information

  • We will not sell your personal information
  • We will not use your information for unrelated marketing outside the Ask AI, site notification, and feedback purposes described here unless you separately agree to that use
  • We will not intentionally share your information with unauthorized third parties

How your information may be stored and managed

Your first name and email address may be stored in the PricZone Ask AI visitor access directory so our administrators can manage access requests, review saved entries, remove entries when needed, and process unsubscribe requests.

Unsubscribe and removal

You may unsubscribe from Ask AI emails by using the unsubscribe option provided in our messages when available. When an unsubscribe request is received, PricZone may notify our administrative team so your saved access entry can be reviewed and removed. Once your saved entry is removed, you may submit the access form again.

Data protection

PricZone takes reasonable steps to manage visitor information within the website tools used for Ask AI access control and communication. However, no online system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your agreement

By submitting the Ask AI form, you confirm that the information you provide is yours or that you are authorized to provide it, and you agree to the collection, storage, and use of that information as described on this page.
